# WIDS Survey Questions for Myanmar Users

To gather meaningful feedback from Myanmar people for your **Wireless Intrusion Detection System (WIDS)** project, your survey should aim to understand their current level of cybersecurity awareness, their network usage habits, and their willingness to adopt a new security tool (especially one involving both hardware like an ESP32 and a desktop app).

Since the target audience is in Myanmar, it's important to keep the language accessible (avoiding overly complex jargon or offering a Burmese translation) and focus on practical, day-to-day security concerns.

## 1. Basic Demographics & Tech Fluency
*To understand who is taking the survey and their comfort level with technology.*
*   **What is your age group?** (e.g., 18-24, 25-34, 35-44, 45+)
*   **What is your primary occupation/field of study?** (e.g., IT/Tech, Student, Business, Non-Tech)
*   **How would you rate your knowledge of computer networks and Wi-Fi?** (1 = Beginner, 5 = Expert)
*   **What operating system do you primarily use on your computer?** (Windows, macOS, Linux, I don't use a computer)

## 2. Cybersecurity Awareness & Experience
*To gauge if they perceive Wi-Fi hacking as a real threat in Myanmar.*
*   **Are you aware that someone connected to the same Wi-Fi network as you can potentially intercept your data or hack your device?** (Yes / No / Unsure)
*   **Have you ever experienced or suspected that your home or office Wi-Fi network was hacked or used by an unauthorized person?** (Yes / No / Unsure)
*   **Which of the following cyber threats are you most concerned about?** (Select all that apply: Phishing, Wi-Fi hacking, Ransomware, Identity theft, Unauthorized network access)

## 3. Current Networking Habits
*To understand the environments where your WIDS would be deployed.*
*   **How often do you connect to public or shared Wi-Fi networks (e.g., at cafes, airports, malls in Myanmar)?** (Daily / Weekly / Rarely / Never)
*   **Do you currently use any security tools to protect your home or office network?** (Select all that apply: Antivirus, VPN, Firewall, Custom Router Settings, None)
*   **Do you know how to check who is connected to your home Wi-Fi network?** (Yes / No)

## 4. Receptiveness to the WIDS Project
*To validate the demand for your specific hybrid WIDS approach (Python App + ESP32).*
*   **If there was an application that could alert you when someone is trying to hack your Wi-Fi or steal your data (like ARP spoofing), how likely are you to use it?** (1 = Not likely, 5 = Very likely)
*   **This project uses a small, external hardware device (ESP32) plugged into your computer to detect advanced wireless threats. Would you be willing to purchase and use an external device for better security?**
    *   Yes, definitely
    *   Yes, if it is affordable (under ~20,000 - 30,000 MMK)
    *   No, I only want software-based solutions
*   **What features would be most important to you in a network security tool?** (Rank in order of importance: Easy-to-understand alerts, detailed technical logs, stopping the attacker automatically, low system resource usage, visually appealing dashboard)

## 5. Localization and Usability (Myanmar Context)
*To ensure the tool is actually usable for the local population.*
*   **In which language would you prefer the application's interface and security alerts to be?** (Myanmar/Burmese only / English only / Both)
*   **If the application detects a threat, how would you prefer to be notified?** (Desktop popup, Sound alarm, Email, Mobile notification)
*   **What is the biggest barrier preventing you from using advanced cybersecurity tools today?** (Too expensive, Too difficult to understand, Lack of awareness, Language barrier)

---

### Tips for conducting the survey:
*   **Provide Context:** Before the first question, give a very brief, non-technical explanation of what your WIDS does. (e.g., *"We are building a tool that acts like an alarm system for your Wi-Fi, alerting you if hackers try to break in."*)
*   **Bilingual Format:** Offer the survey in both Burmese and English to get a broader response rate, especially from non-IT professionals.
*   **Keep it Short:** Limit the survey to 10-15 questions to ensure people actually finish it. Google Forms is a great platform for this.
